Rustic Herb & Root Tart

A comforting, earthy tart with a flaky buttery crust, perfect for a slow Sunday evening.

Prep Time 15m
Cook Time 30m
Yield 4 Slices

Ingredients

Servings:
4

Instructions

  1. 1

    Preheat and Prep

    Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. Unroll the puff pastry onto the parchment paper.

  2. 2

    Score the Pastry

    Using a sharp paring knife, gently score a border about 1 inch from the edge of the pastry rectangle. Do not cut all the way through. Prick the inner rectangle all over with a fork to prevent it from puffing up too much in the center.

  3. 3

    Assemble the Tart

    Sprinkle half of the gruyere cheese evenly within the scored border. Layer the sliced sweet potatoes and red onions slightly overlapping, inside the border. Drizzle lightly with olive oil and sprinkle with fresh thyme, salt, and pepper. Top with remaining cheese.

  4. 4

    Bake to Perfection

    Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until the pastry edges are puffed and deeply golden brown, and the sweet potatoes are tender. Let cool for 5 minutes before slicing and serving.
