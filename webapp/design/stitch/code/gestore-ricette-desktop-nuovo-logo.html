Herb-Crusted Nordic Salmon

A refined yet effortlessly simple approach to roasting salmon. The mustard-herb crust locks in moisture while providing a bright, herbaceous counterpoint to the rich fish. Perfect for an elegant weeknight dinner.

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 15 mins
Yields 4 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

01

Preheat and Prepare

Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper to ensure the delicate fish skin doesn't stick and to make cleanup effortless.

02

Create the Herb Crust

In a small ceramic bowl, whisk together the Dijon mustard, freshly chopped dill, lemon zest, a pinch of sea salt, and black pepper until well combined into a thick paste.

03

Roast to Perfection

Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel. Spread the herb mixture evenly over the top of each fillet. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon flakes easily with a fork but remains slightly translucent in the very center.
